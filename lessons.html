Lesson plans and teaching resources

Two complete English Home Language lessons that I planned and taught during my 2026 teaching practicum at Abbotts College Pretoria East, in two different English teaching areas. Each lesson comes with the learning and teaching support material (LTSM) needed to teach it.

Lesson 1 · Poetry: "The Poet", from A Midsummer Night's Dream

Grade 11 English Home Language · CAPS · Literature: poetry · Term 3, Week 7.

A time-crunch device hunt in groups, then poiesis, two thought experiments and a dilemma: would you trade your imagination for the truth? The lesson my UP mentor lecturer observed (98/100).

Open lesson plan

Lesson 2 · Writing: literary essay preparation, Macbeth Acts 1 and 2

Grade 10 English Home Language · CAPS · Writing and presenting: the literary essay · Term 3, Week 5.

Horses that eat each other, a debate on who is to blame for Duncan's murder, and the PEEL method for building an evidence-based essay paragraph.
