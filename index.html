English Home Language teacher, FET Phase · Pretoria East, Gauteng

English teacher with a background in literature and philosophy and 15 years of university teaching experience. Taught Grade 10 and 11 English Home Language at Abbotts College and English First Additional Language at Hoërskool Garsfontein during PGCE practicums in 2026. Builds lessons on respectful, trusting relationships and lively discussion, treating learners as real thinking partners. Teaches what no machine can do for a learner: reading closely, arguing honestly and thinking in dialogue.

I teach because I have seen what happens when students realise they can think for themselves, and that transformation carries into the rest of their lives. Docendo discimus. By teaching, we learn.
